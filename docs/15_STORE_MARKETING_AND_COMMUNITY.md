# 15 — Store, Marketing, and Community Plan

## One-sentence pitch

**Drift through a rain-soaked Pacific Northwest city, master its hidden routes, and race the ghosts of a scene that forgot why it started.**

## Short store description

Seattle After Dark is a fast, stylish arcade racer built around wet streets, steep hills, dangerous shortcuts, and instant restarts. Learn the Rainline, tune your car, chase rival ghosts, and discover what happened to the legendary driver whose final run changed the city’s racing culture.

## Differentiators

- Seattle/Pacific-Northwest-inspired urban identity;
- rain as visual signature;
- compact mastery-focused courses;
- drift-generated boost;
- story about route knowledge and responsibility;
- modern PC indie scope rather than giant open world.

## Demo

Ideal public demo:
- tutorial;
- Rainline Run;
- one rival event;
- one car;
- limited tuning;
- local time attack.

Allow demo PB/ghost to migrate into full game if feasible.

## Community

Channels:
- Steam discussions;
- Discord optional;
- development blog;
- short handling clips;
- weekly/biweekly ghost challenge during promotion.

## Wishlist beats

1. announcement GIF;
2. Rainline Run gameplay;
3. handling deep-dive;
4. soundtrack reveal;
5. rival reveal;
6. demo festival;
7. release date;
8. launch.

## Avoid marketing too early

Do not announce a release window before:
- vertical slice;
- representative performance;
- content production velocity is understood.

## Review build

Provide:
- press kit;
- clean screenshots;
- trailer;
- feature list;
- controller support;
- accessibility summary;
- approximate campaign length;
- known platform limitations.
